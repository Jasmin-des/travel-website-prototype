About this website

Brisbane & Beyond

Brisbane & Beyond is a travel concept designed for older adults and their family groups who want to explore with comfort, inclusion, and ease. The site focuses on experiences that balance scenic discovery with thoughtful pacing, relaxed movement, and meaningful cultural learning.

Why this concept

This project reflects a slower, more human-centred travel style. It groups experiences into nature and culture, making it easier for visitors to choose the right travel pace and the right kind of experience for their group.

References and inspiration

  • Brisbane Tourism and city visitor information.
